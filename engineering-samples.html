Drainage Review

A private C# add-in source prototype reviews a normalized pipe CSV for adverse/low slope, endpoint cover, and missing or duplicated attributes. Native compilation and testing in OpenRoads remain pending. It does not yet read live drainage objects or analyze hydraulic capacity.

Five new OpenRoads review companions

Corridor review toolkit

Private export-based prototypes: 16 automated tests passed and five sample workflows executed. Native OpenRoads integration and Windows launcher tests remain pending. Samples below contain fictional data, not production CAD runs.

Template Drop Review

Checks sampled template stations, missing names and changes. Not corridor template-library synchronization or end-condition validation.

Corridor Region Coverage

Finds interior coverage gaps and overlap lengths against prior coverage. No expected start/end coverage is inferred; overlaps may be intentional.

Cross-Slope Review

Outward slope = 100 × (outer elevation − inner elevation) / absolute offset width. Negative means falling outward. Checks magnitude only; no crown, drainage direction or accessibility certification.

Feature Definition Audit

Compares explicit actual/expected names case-sensitively. Missing expectations are unchecked, not passing. Does not inspect DGN libraries, symbology or live elements.
